HHO Rocket & Acrylic Wet Cell GeneratorAn experimental prototype featuring a custom 3mm acrylic wet-cell reactor with 12 carbon rod electrodes, inline flashback safety protection, a low-pressure storage vessel, and a wireless Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W ignition controller driving a curved nozzle rocket engine.1. System OverviewWet Cell Reactor: 3mm acrylic body housing 12 carbon rod electrodes in a Sodium Hydroxide ($\text{NaOH}$) bath.Safety & Storage: Inline flashback arrestor protecting the storage vessel and reactor during gas transfer.Avionics & Propulsion: Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W controlling a high-voltage arc ignition system driving exhaust through a converging-diverging curved nozzle.2. Fuel Generation (Acrylic Arc Reactor)The electrolyzer reactor uses a sealed 3mm acrylic chamber designed for a submerged wet-cell setup.3mm Acrylic Housing: Custom sealed chamber engineered to hold the liquid electrolyte bath and collect gas in the headspace.Electrolyte Solution: Distilled water mixed with Sodium Hydroxide ($\text{NaOH}$) to lower internal resistance and maximize DC current flow.12 Carbon Rod Array: A total of 12 carbon rods fully submerged in the fluid, wired in parallel ($6\times \text{positive}, 6\times \text{negative}$) to split water into stoichiometric gas ($2\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow 2\text{H}_2 + \text{O}_2$).3. Gas Storage & Flashback SafetyTo prevent accidental flame propagation back to the generator, an inline flashback arrestor is positioned directly between the reactor line and the fuel storage unit.Inline Flashback Arrestor: A mechanical flame arrestor integrated into the output line that quenches flame fronts using a micro-porous metallic barrier before fire can reach the acrylic reactor.Low-Pressure Storage Tank: Receives raw HHO gas safely and holds the fuel volume under controlled low pressure.Check Valve Assembly: Mechanically seals the stored gas inside the tank, maintaining holding pressure prior to launch.4. Wireless Avionics & Rocket IgnitionIgnition is triggered remotely using a micro-controller setup connected to high-voltage step-up electronics.Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W: Configured as a wireless access point/node. It receives a secure remote trigger signal over Wi-Fi/Bluetooth to send a high GPIO pulse.High-Voltage Arc Driver: The GPIO pin triggers a relay/MOSFET circuit connected to a high-voltage pulse module, generating a high-temperature electric arc across internal terminals inside the combustion chamber.Curved Converging-Diverging Nozzle:Compression: Smoothly curved nozzle walls compress hot expanding water vapor down to a narrow throat section, building peak internal chamber pressure.Decompression: The nozzle widens out after the throat, allowing compressed exhaust to decompress and accelerate out at supersonic velocity to generate forward thrust.5. Operational Safety SummaryDual Barrier Protection: The combination of an inline flashback arrestor and a physical mechanical valve prevents flame flashback into the acrylic reactor.Wireless Standoff Distance: Using the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W allows remote arming and ignition from a safe distance, keeping operators clear of high-pressure combustion zones.
